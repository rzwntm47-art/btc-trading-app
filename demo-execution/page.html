How this demo works

Gainers are checked first for BUY and losers for SELL using the eligible USDⓈ-M Futures demo 24-hour ranking. Other USDT perpetuals keep rotating, and earlier coins are checked again. A ranking change of 10%, 20% or 30% describes past movement, not future profit or win probability. The scanner accepts either a confirmed breakout or continuing momentum on closed 1–5m, 10m, 15m, 20m, 30m or 60m candles. Momentum entries require aligned 5/20-period averages and a confirming 5m trend, a candle body of at least 40%, directional close strength of at least 65%, and volume of at least 0.8× the recent average. Reversing candles and moves extended more than two recent ATRs are skipped. Breakouts retain their candle and volume checks without requiring the strongest agreement tier. Fresh 1m direction, quotes, fees and entry capacity are checked before entry. The commission screen uses the selected setup timeframe: two observed ATRs must exceed three times estimated round-trip fees per unit. All qualifying same-direction timeframes are retained; if the earliest one fails price, freshness or commission checks, the next candidate is considered before any order is submitted. A longer-timeframe setup still needs fresh 1m direction and volatility checks, but its fee screen is not limited to a 1m move. This is a cost screen, not a forecast.

New trades target $300 margin. Candle agreement selects a leverage ceiling of 3×, 5× or 10×; mixed, unavailable or opposing BTC 5m market direction limits that ceiling to 3×. Observed volatility, fees, price ticks and Binance limits can reduce it further, down to 1×. The existing $20 volatility-and-fee allowance still controls entry sizing; it is not an exit or a maximum loss. No stop-loss price is calculated for new trades. Lower leverage means less position exposure for the same margin. Entry sizing does not top up existing positions; the loss rule may reduce them 25% once. The strategy has not been validated for profitability and does not predict the next candle.

The top 10 eligible gainers and 10 losers are refreshed once per minute. Eight rotating mover slots are followed by recent candidates and four full-market slots, with up to three public checks at a time. Completed candles are cached until their next boundary; executable bid/ask and mark prices are rechecked before dispatch. Entry prices may move up to 0.75 of the selected candle ATR from its close. Breakouts may retest their boundary by up to 0.10 ATR while fresh directional confirmation remains valid. Momentum setups must remain beyond their trend boundary. Larger reversals, excessive extension, stale prices, unworkable spreads, fee burdens or exchange limits skip that entry. Temporary candidate-read failures and definite price-filter rejections continue scanning other coins. Unknown order status, access errors and rate limits require resolution. Qualified signals do not guarantee fills or profit.

Uses available USDT within a $5,000 total margin budget, a $300 target margin per entry. Quantity rounds up to Binance’s lot step; leverage adapts to recent volatility and fees, then respects Binance leverage or capacity limits. If available margin including fees or total margin room is insufficient, no smaller order is sent. Market fill prices and partial fills can change actual entry margin; the bot does not automatically top up a fill. Entry sizing does not top up existing positions; the loss rule may reduce them 25% once. Estimated round-trip commission is shown with each position. The entry commission screen remains active; actual fees may differ. Market orders may incur spread and slippage. Orders respect Binance quantity, notional and leverage limits. No fixed position-count cap; one per symbol, within available collateral and the total margin budget. Different symbols can enter consecutively after checks, with serialized submissions. The same symbol has a 10-minute cooldown after close. Cross margin shares collateral.

Existing bot-managed positions adopt these exits when the updated engine synchronizes. Only this bot’s journaled old stop-loss, take-profit and profit-floor orders are cancelled. The new policy uses fresh executable bid/ask prices, or weighted order-book depth when needed, to estimate closing net after opening and expected closing taker fees. If that estimate is positive, the engine rechecks it and requests a full reduce-only close without waiting for $20 or a pullback. This estimate covers the remaining quantity and excludes funding; already realized reductions and fees appear in session income. It can differ from Binance’s close dialog and the final fill.

When a position’s estimated net is negative, the engine reduces its current quantity by 25% once, rounded down to Binance’s quantity step. Fees alone can trigger this soon after entry. A quantity too small to reduce within exchange limits is skipped and reported. A rejected reduction is reported without repeated submissions; uncertain outcomes are reconciled before another exit. The reduction realizes part of the loss and releases some margin; it does not recover losses. No loss timer or stop-loss applies to the remainder. These exits require the engine running; they are not standing exchange take-profit orders.

At $100 account-wide net session profit, new entries stop and all bot-managed positions receive reduce-only close requests, including losing positions. Closure remains latched even if the result falls below $100 while closing. Pending partial reductions are resolved before full closes. Unmanaged positions are not closed. The final booked result is not guaranteed to be exactly $100. Released margin can support new entries only when the existing signal, fee, capacity and cooldown checks pass.
