Activity & connection
How this demo works

V1.67 keeps Auto enabled while new entries wait for recoverable errors or reconciliation. Open-position checks run before account income checks and during entry-only retry delays. Pending orders and unverified protection still block new entries; exchange-wide rate limits delay all requests. Manual Stop and the $100 session stop remain. Conditional-order ID recovery is preserved. Temporary read failures wait and retry automatically; startup checks must finish before entries resume. Manual Stop cancels retries. Saved stale stops are cleared only after Binance confirms the trade is flat or replacement loss protection is active. No reset or history deletion is needed.

The top 20 eligible USDⓈ-M Futures demo gainers are LONG candidates; the top 20 losers are SHORT candidates. Rankings refresh once per minute. Eight rotating candidates and recent qualifying setups repeat continuously, with up to three public checks at once. Rankings describe past 24-hour changes, not future profit. Other coins are not traded by this strategy.

Independently check completed 1–10 minute candles, plus 15m, 20m, 30m and 60m candles with Bollinger Bands (20-period simple average, two population standard deviations) and Williams %R (14 periods). A LONG needs a lower-band touch on the last two candles, Williams %R at or below -80 on one of those candles, then a rising bullish close back above the lower band with improving %R. SHORT uses the reverse: upper-band touch, %R at or above -20, and a falling bearish close back below the upper band. Williams %R measures price within its recent high-low range; it is not a percentage of buyers or sellers. A band touch alone is not an entry. The 2–4m and 6–10m candles use complete UTC-aligned 1m groups; longer frames use complete 5m groups. Each timeframe is independent. Only two shared candle datasets are needed per coin.

Do not chase a setup that has already reached the middle band. Check current quotes, spread, fees, exchange limits and fresh 1m direction before entry. A 1m entry revalidates its Bollinger reversal. Longer entries allow a small 1m pullback but reject a sharp or sustained reversal. Estimated opening and closing commission must be at most 25% of the gross middle-band reference move, with at least $5 net at that reference and current round-trip commission within half the normal $6 loss budget. If the first timeframe fails sizing, other qualifying timeframes are checked against the same fresh quote. Confirmed commission rates are cached for at most one minute; unavailable or expired fees block that entry without being guessed. Rejected candidates do not turn Auto off. Temporary read failures retry; unknown order outcomes must be reconciled before more entries.

Each new trade targets $300 USDT margin, rounded up to Binance’s quantity step, within a $5,000 total margin budget. Use the highest Binance-accepted leverage that permits the full position size and leaves room for the $6 after-fee stop. Lower leverage when needed for fees, spread and tick size. A notional or quantity limit can require lower leverage; the app does not shrink the margin to fit. Insufficient margin plus fees skips the entry. If no supported leverage leaves room for the $6 after-fee loss target, skip and show the reason. Market fills can change actual margin. Existing positions are not topped up or releveraged. One position per symbol; ten-minute cooldown after a close. Cross positions share USDT collateral.

Estimated total trade net = realized partial-close P&L − commissions already paid + funding + remaining P&L at an executable closing price − estimated closing commission. Matched Binance execution history is required. This differs from Binance unrealized P&L, which excludes costs. Unknown costs defer profit exits rather than being treated as zero.

Profit exits use total net after fees. No profit floor is armed at $5 or below. Once profit exceeds $5, arm a $5 floor; above $8, raise it to $8; above $11, raise it to $11; continue in $3 steps without a profit cap. Hold while profit rises, then close when a fresh net estimate returns to or below the armed floor. For example, a peak of exactly $20 arms $17; a peak above $20 arms $20. The floor never moves down and survives an engine restart. A gap through a floor triggers an exit at the available market price, even if the result is below the intended floor. Old Bollinger profit targets no longer close trades.

At -$6 total net, close unless fresh 1m and 5m trends, a strong completed 1m candle since entry, and the current executable price support recovery. Recheck that evidence on each loss evaluation; if it weakens or is unavailable, close at the current price. Never extend the target beyond -$12. Install a Binance STOP_MARKET backup targeting -$12, adjusted for paid fees and booked results. The normal -$6 decision and profit floors require the engine running; the exchange backup remains independently active. No loss timer or partial-loss reduction. Existing bot positions migrate without changing size or leverage; the new stop is confirmed before older bot exits are cancelled. Earlier partial-close losses still count. Gaps, slippage, network delays and fees can exceed either target; recovery is not guaranteed.

At $100 estimated account-wide net after expected closing fees, close all bot positions, including losers, and stop new entries. The decision remains latched while unresolved orders are reconciled. Unmanaged positions are not closed. Final booked net can differ. Updating the website does not update a running laptop connector: replace its files and restart it to load V1.67.
