How this demo works

The $100 goal over 2–3 hours is aspirational. No deadline forces entries, and profitability has not been established by backtesting or live validation.

Prioritizes the top 10 positive and top 10 negative 24-hour percentage movers among eligible trading USDT-margined perpetuals, refreshed at most once per minute. Priority gainers require BUY confirmation; priority losers require SELL confirmation. Four rotating mover slots per scan are followed by recent-candidate rechecks and at least eight full-market cursor slots, so all coins continue to be checked. Rankings come from Futures demo market data and may differ from Binance Spot or another selected list period. Ranking is not a profitability signal. Each coin is evaluated on closed 1-minute candles, then complete 2-minute, 3-minute, 4-minute and 5-minute candles, followed by 10-, 15-, 20-, 30- and 60-minute candles if earlier timeframes do not qualify. Longer candles use complete UTC-aligned groups of 5-minute candles; the existing 5-minute trend confirmation remains. Selective entries require a confirming 5-minute trend, volume at least 1.5× its recent average, body at least 50% of candle range, and close strength at least 75%. Additionally, volume must reach 2× average or the candle must have at least 90% close strength and 70% body. A two-ATR movement must exceed three times estimated round-trip commission per unit; this is a cost screen, not a price forecast. BUY and SELL are supported. New entries target $200 margin before trading fees. Before each entry, fresh closed 1m candles must still confirm the trade direction, with a directional body, close location and 5/20-period mean alignment. This measures observed momentum; it does not predict the next 30 seconds. Leverage is 25× when allowed, otherwise the highest accepted value below 25× that fits Binance’s position-capacity limit. Recent 1m movement must also clear the existing commission screen. Immediately before dispatch, fresh best bid/ask and mark prices are checked. Entries are skipped if the quoted spread plus estimated fees already reaches the $8 loss target, or the current mark would trigger the planned stop. These quotes are estimates: market depth, fills and later prices can differ. No order is forced if those constraints cannot fit. The $200 × 25× position has the same $5,000 exposure as $100 × 50×, so this sizing change alone does not reduce fees. Entry checks have not been shown to make this strategy profitable. Existing positions retain their leverage. No trade is guaranteed profitable and these rules have not been validated by profitability backtesting.

Uses available USDT within a $5,000 total margin budget, a $200 target margin per entry. Quantity rounds up to Binance’s lot step; leverage is reduced only for Binance leverage or capacity limits. If available margin including fees or total margin room is insufficient, no smaller order is sent. Market fill prices and partial fills can change actual entry margin; the bot does not automatically top up a fill. Existing positions are not resized. Estimated round-trip commission is shown with each position. If fees already reach the $8 loss target, no new entry is sent; actual fees may differ. The $8 planned stop remains. Market orders may incur spread and slippage. Orders respect Binance quantity, notional and leverage limits. No fixed position-count cap; one per symbol, within available collateral and the total margin budget. At least 60 seconds between filled entries and 10 minutes before re-entering a closed symbol. Cross margin shares collateral.

Existing bot-owned positions adopt the new exit rules on reconciliation. A replacement $8 exchange stop is confirmed before old exits are cancelled. If the loss threshold is already breached, a reduce-only close is attempted. Existing positions keep their leverage. There is no timed loss exit. Positions close when estimated net loss reaches $8; actual execution can differ because of fees, polling delays and slippage.

No recovery extension: all bot positions use an $8 planned net loss stop. On connection, any saved $12 stop is replaced by a confirmed $8 stop before the old stop is cancelled. If the tighter threshold is already reached, a reduce-only close is attempted. Profit rules are unchanged.

Profit can keep growing. No profit floor applies until observed net profit exceeds $4. Above $4 activates a $4 floor; above $8 activates $8; above $16 activates $16; above $32 activates $32, then floors increase by $5 ($37, $42, $47…). Exactly reaching a new milestone does not activate it until profit exceeds it. A pullback to or below the floor triggers a market close. The floor never decreases. Manual close remains available. Profit floors require this page open and connected; the exchange stop remains active after page closure. Polling, outages, fees, funding and slippage can change realized results.

At an observed $100 net session result, the bot closes its own positions and stops new entries. Final realized profit may be below $100 because of closing fees, slippage or delays. The target stop stays locked across reloads and midnight until you explicitly select New $100 session while flat. Manual positions are not closed. Unless the target stop is locked, the Dubai trading day resets at midnight. Daily P&L includes USDT realized trading P&L, commissions, funding and the change in open P&L from the day’s first account snapshot. Unobserved midnight changes can affect attribution. Credentials and trade history are retained. No live trading, withdrawals or asset conversions.
