How this demo works

The top 20 eligible USDⓈ-M Futures demo gainers are LONG candidates; the top 20 losers are SHORT candidates. Rankings refresh once per minute. Eight rotating candidates and recent qualifying setups repeat continuously, with up to three public checks at once. Rankings describe past 24-hour changes, not future profit. Other coins are not traded by this strategy.

Use completed 5-minute candles with Bollinger Bands (20-period simple average, two population standard deviations) and Williams %R (14 periods). A LONG needs a lower-band touch on the last two candles, Williams %R at or below -80 on one of those candles, then a rising bullish close back above the lower band with improving %R. SHORT uses the reverse: upper-band touch, %R at or above -20, and a falling bearish close back below the upper band. Williams %R measures price within its recent high-low range; it is not a percentage of buyers or sellers. A band touch alone is not an entry.

Do not chase a setup that has already reached the middle band. Check current quotes, spread, fees, exchange limits and fresh 1m direction before entry. A small 1m pullback is allowed, but a sharp or sustained reversal is rejected. The middle-band distance must exceed twice the estimated round-trip fee per unit. Rejected candidates do not turn Auto off. Temporary read failures retry; unknown order outcomes must be reconciled before more entries.

Each trade uses up to $300 USDT margin within a $5,000 total margin budget. Quantity is rounded down and sized so an adverse move of 1.5 recent 5m ATRs plus estimated fees fits the $2 loss target. Estimated round-trip fees are limited to $1 at entry. This often means using much less than $300. Choose the lowest integer leverage needed for that size, within Binance limits and a 150× ceiling; never increase size just to use more leverage. One position per symbol; ten-minute cooldown after a close. Cross positions share USDT collateral. No automatic adding to losing positions.

Estimated total trade net = realized partial-close P&L − commissions already paid + funding + remaining P&L at an executable closing price − estimated closing commission. Matched Binance execution history is required. This differs from Binance unrealized P&L, which excludes costs. Unknown costs defer profit exits rather than being treated as zero.

At the middle band, close only when the fresh total net estimate is positive. Continue toward the opposite outer band only if a fresh closed 1m candle after entry confirms momentum beyond the middle and 5m Williams %R supports that direction. The continuation target is 90% of the distance from the middle to the opposite outer band. Once extended, freeze those targets: close before the outer band or on a pullback to the middle, while estimated total net remains positive. Before extension, targets follow fresh closed 5m bands. Recheck price and paid/estimated fees before submitting any profit close. There is no guaranteed profit or exact fill.

Close the full remaining bot position at -$2 estimated total trade net. Install a Binance STOP_MARKET backup, adjusted for paid fees and booked results. No timed loss rule and no 25% loss trim. Confirm the replacement stop before cancelling older bot exits. Existing bot positions migrate to these rules when the updated engine reconciles them; previous partial-close losses still count. Stops trigger on mark price; gaps, slippage, network delays and execution fees can exceed $2.

At $100 estimated account-wide net after expected closing fees, close all bot positions, including losers, and stop new entries. The decision remains latched while unresolved orders are reconciled. Unmanaged positions are not closed. Final booked net can differ. Updating the website does not update a running laptop connector: replace its files and restart it to load V1.59.
