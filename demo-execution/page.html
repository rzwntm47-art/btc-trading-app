How this demo works

Start Demo Auto scans eligible crypto perpetuals in rotating batches. A closed 5-minute candle must break the previous 20-candle range; stale signals and chased prices are skipped. This rules-based strategy has not demonstrated profitability. Starting does not guarantee an immediate entry or $100 profit.

Entries are market orders. Each confirmed position receives Binance conditional stop-market and take-profit-market exits. Above estimated $10 net profit, the page adds a $10 profit-floor stop. Stop Auto prevents new entries; it keeps managing existing bot positions. Close sends a reduce-only order. Other positions are not adopted or automatically closed.

Keep this page open and visible for new entries and profit-floor updates. Accepted exchange protective orders remain on Binance after disconnect or closure. A session goal cannot be enforced while the page is closed. Do not close the page during an order submission; review Binance directly if a connection fails.

Use a dedicated demo account without other orders or trading sessions. The account session goal includes all USDT trading income and open USDT P&L after the session starts. Wallet transfers and changes in BTC/USDC value are excluded. No withdrawals, asset conversions or live-account endpoints are available.
