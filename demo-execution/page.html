How this demo works

Gainers are checked first for BUY and losers for SELL using the eligible USDⓈ-M Futures demo 24-hour ranking. Other USDT perpetuals keep rotating, and earlier coins are checked again. A ranking change of 10%, 20% or 30% describes past movement, not future profit or win probability. The scanner accepts either a confirmed breakout or continuing momentum on closed 1–5m, 10m, 15m, 20m, 30m or 60m candles. Momentum entries require aligned 5/20-period averages and a confirming 5m trend, a candle body of at least 40%, directional close strength of at least 65%, and volume of at least 0.8× the recent average. Reversing candles and moves extended more than two recent ATRs are skipped. Breakouts retain their candle and volume checks without requiring the strongest agreement tier. Fresh 1m direction, quotes, fees and stop capacity are checked before entry. The existing commission screen remains: two observed ATRs must exceed three times estimated round-trip fees per unit. This is a cost screen, not a forecast.

New trades target $300 margin. Candle agreement selects a leverage ceiling of 3×, 5× or 10×; mixed, unavailable or opposing BTC 5m market direction limits that ceiling to 3×. Observed volatility, fees, price ticks and Binance limits can reduce it further, down to 1×. The plan must leave room for one recent 1m ATR and estimated round-trip fees inside the $8 loss target. Lower leverage means less position exposure for the same margin. Existing positions are not resized. The strategy has not been validated for profitability and does not predict the next candle.

The top 10 eligible gainers and 10 losers are refreshed once per minute. Eight rotating mover slots are followed by recent candidates and four full-market slots, with up to three public checks at a time. Completed candles are cached until their next boundary; executable bid/ask and mark prices are rechecked before dispatch. An adverse reversal, excessive extension, stale price, unworkable spread, fee burden or exchange limit skips that entry. Qualified signals do not guarantee fills or profit.

Uses available USDT within a $5,000 total margin budget, a $300 target margin per entry. Quantity rounds up to Binance’s lot step; leverage adapts to recent volatility and fees, then respects Binance leverage or capacity limits. If available margin including fees or total margin room is insufficient, no smaller order is sent. Market fill prices and partial fills can change actual entry margin; the bot does not automatically top up a fill. Existing positions are not resized. Estimated round-trip commission is shown with each position. If fees already reach the $8 loss target, no new entry is sent; actual fees may differ. The $8 planned stop remains. Market orders may incur spread and slippage. Orders respect Binance quantity, notional and leverage limits. No fixed position-count cap; one per symbol, within available collateral and the total margin budget. At least 60 seconds between filled entries and 10 minutes before re-entering a closed symbol. Cross margin shares collateral.

Existing bot-owned positions adopt the new exit rules on reconciliation. A replacement $8 exchange stop is confirmed before old exits are cancelled. If the loss threshold is already breached, a reduce-only close is attempted. Existing positions keep their leverage. There is no timed loss exit. Positions close when estimated net loss reaches $8; actual execution can differ because of fees, polling delays and slippage.

No recovery extension: all bot positions use an $8 planned net loss stop. On connection, any saved $12 stop is replaced by a confirmed $8 stop before the old stop is cancelled. If the tighter threshold is already reached, a reduce-only close is attempted. Profit rules are unchanged.

Profit can keep growing. No profit floor applies until observed net profit exceeds $4. Above $4 activates a $4 floor; above $8 activates $8; above $16 activates $16; above $32 activates $32, then floors increase by $5 ($37, $42, $47…). Exactly reaching a new milestone does not activate it until profit exceeds it. A pullback to or below the floor triggers a market close. The floor never decreases. Manual close remains available. Profit floors require the browser page open in browser mode, or the laptop connector running in background mode; the exchange stop remains active after page closure. Polling, outages, fees, funding and slippage can change realized results.

At an observed $100 net session result, the bot closes its own positions and stops new entries. Final realized profit may be below $100 because of closing fees, slippage or delays. The target stop stays locked across reloads and midnight until you explicitly select New $100 session while flat. Manual positions are not closed. Unless the target stop is locked, the Dubai trading day resets at midnight. Daily P&L includes USDT realized trading P&L, commissions, funding and the change in open P&L from the day’s first account snapshot. Unobserved midnight changes can affect attribution. Credentials and trade history are retained. No live trading, withdrawals or asset conversions.
