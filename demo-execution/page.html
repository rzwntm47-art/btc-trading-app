How this demo works

Continuously scans all listed trading USDT-margined perpetuals, with priority rechecks of recent candidates. Each coin is evaluated on closed 1-minute candles, then complete 2-minute, 3-minute and 4-minute candles if earlier timeframes do not qualify. Breakouts require a confirming 5-minute trend, volume at least 1.2× its recent average, and candle-body confirmation. BUY and SELL are supported. New entries request 50× leverage; if Binance rejects 50×, lower leverage is tried (25×, 20×, 10×, 5×, 3×, 2×, 1×). New 50× entries target at least $100 margin (about $5,000 exposure), rounded to the exchange quantity step. Lower leverage uses proportionally less margin, subject to available funds. Existing positions retain their leverage. No trade is guaranteed profitable and these rules have not been validated by profitability backtesting.

Uses available USDT: $100 margin at 50×, $50 at 25×, $40 at 20×, scaled down further if funds are limited below 50×. The $8 planned stop includes estimated fees; bigger positions leave less room for adverse price movement. Entries are skipped if estimated round-trip fees consume the loss allowance. Orders respect Binance quantity, notional and leverage limits. No arbitrary position-count cap; one bot position per symbol. Cross margin shares collateral.

Existing bot-owned positions adopt the new exit rules on reconciliation. A replacement $8 exchange stop is confirmed before old exits are cancelled. If the loss threshold is already breached, a reduce-only close is attempted. Existing positions keep their leverage. There is no timed loss exit. Positions close when estimated net loss reaches $8; actual execution can differ because of fees, polling delays and slippage.

Profit can keep growing. No profit floor applies until observed net profit exceeds $4. Above $4 activates a $4 floor; above $8 activates $8; above $16 activates $16; above $32 activates $32, then floors increase by $5 ($37, $42, $47…). Exactly reaching a new milestone does not activate it until profit exceeds it. A pullback to or below the floor triggers a market close. The floor never decreases. Manual close remains available. Profit floors require this page open and connected; the exchange stop remains active after page closure. Polling, outages, fees, funding and slippage can change realized results.

The $100 target is daily progress, not a guarantee or stop-trading limit. The Dubai trading day resets at midnight. Daily P&L includes USDT realized trading P&L, commissions, funding and the change in open P&L from the day’s first account snapshot. Unobserved midnight changes can affect attribution. Credentials and trade history are retained. No live trading, withdrawals or asset conversions.
