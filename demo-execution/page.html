How this demo works

Start Demo Auto repeatedly scans every eligible crypto perpetual in rotating batches. Recent breakout candidates are also rechecked in priority slots after 30 seconds when available, without waiting for the full cycle. Moderate confirmation uses 3× leverage. The latest result per coin includes entry or skip reasons. If there is no closed 1-minute breakout, the app checks complete 2-minute candles built from paired 1-minute candles. A qualifying breakout must agree with the 5-minute trend, have at least 1.2× recent average volume, and close strongly within its candle. Weak, stale and chased signals are skipped. Rule agreement selects 3×, 5× or 8× leverage; this score is not a win probability. Size is reduced so the $5 planned loss allows at least 1.5 ATR of price movement including estimated fees. No fixed bot-position count: the margin budget and exchange limits govern entries. Both BUY and SELL signals are supported; one position per symbol in One-Way Mode. These experimental filters have not been backtested for profitability. This rules-based strategy has not demonstrated profitability. Starting does not guarantee an immediate entry or $100 profit.

Entries are market orders. New positions receive a Binance stop-market exit targeting a $5 loss including estimated fees. Profit can keep growing beyond 60 seconds. At an observed estimated net peak of $1 the app arms a $0.50 floor; at $2 it raises the floor to $1; at $3 to $2, then $1 higher for each whole dollar of peak profit. The floor never decreases. A pullback to or below the floor triggers a reduce-only market close. Manual closure remains available. The rising floor is enforced by the page, not an exchange trailing order; keep the page open and connected. Actual fills may differ from the floor. The app rotates advisory reviews of closed 1m and 5m candles for profitable positions; each card shows the result and timestamp. This is trend evidence, not a prediction. The loss timer starts at the first check showing estimated net loss, including estimated entry and exit fees. It resets at net break-even or profit. A later loss starts a fresh 60-second timer. The app closes on the first losing check at or after 60 seconds from that loss start, or at the planned dollar loss threshold, whichever is detected first. Trade age does not start the loss timer. Older positions retain their original exit policy. Stop Auto prevents new entries; it keeps managing existing bot positions. Close sends a reduce-only order. Other positions are not adopted or automatically closed.

Keep this page open and visible for new entries and 60-second loss checks. Accepted exchange protective orders remain on Binance after disconnect or closure. The 60-second check requires the page open and connected and can be delayed by requests or outages. Stop fills can exceed $5 due to slippage. The $100 session goal pauses new entries; new positions continue under their profit-floor and loss rules. Do not close the page during an order submission; review Binance directly if a connection fails.

Use a dedicated demo account without other orders or trading sessions. The account session goal includes all USDT trading income and open USDT P&L after the session starts. Wallet transfers and changes in BTC/USDC value are excluded. No withdrawals, asset conversions or live-account endpoints are available.
