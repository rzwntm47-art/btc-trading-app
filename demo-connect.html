How this demo works

Continuously scans all listed trading USDT-margined perpetuals, with priority rechecks of recent candidates. Each coin is evaluated on closed 1-minute candles, then complete 2-minute, 3-minute, 4-minute and 5-minute candles if earlier timeframes do not qualify. Breakouts require a confirming 5-minute trend, volume at least 1.2× its recent average, and candle-body confirmation. BUY and SELL are supported. New entries request 50× leverage; if Binance rejects 50×, lower leverage is tried (25×, 20×, 10×, 5×, 3×, 2×, 1×). Position size is limited by available margin, the $8 stop at a volatility-based distance, and an estimated $2 round-trip commission budget. Smaller 50× positions are allowed. Existing positions retain their leverage. No trade is guaranteed profitable and these rules have not been validated by profitability backtesting.

Uses available USDT up to $100 margin per entry, with no minimum margin requirement. Quantity adjusts to volatility and fees. Estimated round-trip commission is capped at $2 when sizing; actual commission can differ. The $8 planned stop remains. Market orders may incur spread and slippage. Orders respect Binance quantity, notional and leverage limits. No arbitrary position-count cap; one bot position per symbol. Cross margin shares collateral.

Existing bot-owned positions adopt the new exit rules on reconciliation. A replacement $8 exchange stop is confirmed before old exits are cancelled. If the loss threshold is already breached, a reduce-only close is attempted. Existing positions keep their leverage. There is no timed loss exit. Positions close when estimated net loss reaches $8; actual execution can differ because of fees, polling delays and slippage.

Recovery extension (demo only): while estimated net loss is between $6 and $8 and improving versus a recent observation, fresh closed 1m candle direction, body and volume must agree with a 5m trend. A fresh position check must still show improvement before $8 is breached. Only then is a $12 stop installed and confirmed before removing the $8 stop. This may happen once per trade. Failed checks retain $8; missing protective orders pause entries and attempt closure. Recovery is not guaranteed; gaps and slippage can exceed either planned stop.

Profit can keep growing. No profit floor applies until observed net profit exceeds $4. Above $4 activates a $4 floor; above $8 activates $8; above $16 activates $16; above $32 activates $32, then floors increase by $5 ($37, $42, $47…). Exactly reaching a new milestone does not activate it until profit exceeds it. A pullback to or below the floor triggers a market close. The floor never decreases. Manual close remains available. Profit floors require this page open and connected; the exchange stop remains active after page closure. Polling, outages, fees, funding and slippage can change realized results.

At an observed $100 net session result, the bot closes its own positions and stops new entries. Final realized profit may be below $100 because of closing fees, slippage or delays. The target stop stays locked across reloads and midnight until you explicitly select New $100 session while flat. Manual positions are not closed. Unless the target stop is locked, the Dubai trading day resets at midnight. Daily P&L includes USDT realized trading P&L, commissions, funding and the change in open P&L from the day’s first account snapshot. Unobserved midnight changes can affect attribution. Credentials and trade history are retained. No live trading, withdrawals or asset conversions.
